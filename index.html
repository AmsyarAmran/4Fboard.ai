Welcome to 4Fboard.ai

Choose a learning mode, enter a topic or question, then create your board. You can draw, save your lesson and export your board.

  • Topic — explore an idea from the beginning.
  • Exam prep — organise revision around key points.
  • Homework — work through a question step by step.

This preview uses local demo content. Connect an AI service for generated lessons.
